Privacy

ColorMerge is designed to be playable without creating an account or entering personal information. The game itself does not ask for your name, email address, or payment information.

Advertising

ColorMerge may offer optional rewarded advertising. If Google AdSense H5 Games Ads is active, Google and its partners may use cookies, device information, IP addresses, and similar technologies to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. An ad may not be available for every player or every request.

You can learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services in Google’s partner-sites privacy explanation.

Your choice

Rewarded ads are optional. You may decline or close the offer and restart the game instead. A heart is granted only when the rewarded message or ad is completed; clicking an advertiser’s link is never required.

Direct sponsor links

A sponsor message may include a link that opens another website or your email application. Those services have their own privacy practices, and ColorMerge does not control them.
