ABOUT THE GAME

Mix your way to a match.

ColorMerge is a compact browser game about visual judgment, memory, and the surprising results of combining simple colors. Your target fills the screen. Your mixture lives in the center. The challenge is to make them match before your hearts run out.

How it works

  1. Choose from blue, red, yellow, white, and black.
  2. Each press adds one color to the center mixture.
  3. The dots show how many colors make up the target.
  4. Match early to earn a bonus heart, or recover after a loss with an optional rewarded ad.

Why the levels change

Each level adds another color to the hidden recipe. The possible mixtures become more nuanced as the sequence grows, so a simple first round gradually turns into a longer exercise in color recognition.
